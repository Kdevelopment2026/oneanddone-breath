THREE TECHNIQUES THAT WORK
• Box breathing (4-4-4-4) — a steady, general-purpose reset for stress and nerves.
• 4-7-8 breathing — a longer hold and slow exhale, popular for winding down before sleep.
• Coherent breathing (5.5 in / 5.5 out) — even, unhurried breathing for calm and focus.

PRESETS FOR REAL MOMENTS
• Before a meeting — a quick, steady reset.
• Wind down — slower, longer exhale.
• Reset focus — even, unhurried breathing.
Each preset picks a sensible technique and length. Change either whenever you like.

A GUIDE YOU CAN FOLLOW WITHOUT THINKING
• A luminous breathing ring that swells as you breathe in and settles as you breathe out.
• "Breathe in", "Hold", "Breathe out" — always in plain words, never just an animation.
• A countdown for each phase and the time left in your session.
• Sessions of 1, 2, 3, 5 or 10 minutes.
• A soft chime and a gentle tap on each phase change — both optional.
• The screen stays on for the whole session.
• Pause and resume. If you leave the app, the session waits for you.
