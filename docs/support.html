The techniques

Box breathing — in for 4, hold 4, out for 4, hold 4. A steady, general-purpose reset.
4-7-8 — in for 4, hold 7, out for 8. A longer hold and slow exhale, popular for winding down.
Coherent breathing — in for 5.5, out for 5.5, no holds. The simplest place to start.

Common questions

Is there a subscription? No. You paid once when you downloaded the app and that is the only charge there will ever be. Every technique is included.

Where's my data? There isn't any. Nothing about your sessions is saved. See the privacy policy.

The screen went dark mid-session. The app keeps the screen awake while a session is running. If it still dims, check Settings → Display & Brightness on your iPhone.

No sound or vibration on phase changes. Open the app's settings (the icon top-right on the home screen) and check that Sound and Haptics are on. The chime also respects your ringer switch.

I left the app during a session. The session pauses. Come back and tap Resume.

Is this medical advice? No. These are well-known breathing patterns for calm and focus. If you have a breathing or heart condition, or feel light-headed, stop and speak to a doctor.
