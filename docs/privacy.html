OneAndDone Breath privacy policy

Last updated 13 September 2026

OneAndDone Breath is a breathing app for iPhone. It is built so that there is nothing to collect. This page says what that means in practice.

What OneAndDone Breath collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no sign-in, no server and no network access. The App Store privacy label for OneAndDone Breath is Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

Two preferences — whether sound and haptics are on — are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Nothing about your breathing sessions is recorded, on the device or anywhere else: no history, no streaks, no timestamps.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

None. OneAndDone Breath contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code and no third-party software development kits that talk to the internet.

Purchases

You buy the app once from the App Store. There is no subscription and no in-app purchase, so the app never handles payment details — Apple does, under Apple's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the two stored preferences. There is nothing else to delete.

Children

OneAndDone Breath does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

Changes

If a future version ever adds a feature that sends data anywhere, this policy will be updated first and the change will be clearly described in the app.
